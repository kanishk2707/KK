Kanishk
=======

To put Kanishk on this computer:

    Double-click  Setup.bat

That is all. It takes about half a minute.

You do not need to install anything else - no internet, no admin password,
no Node, nothing. Everything he needs is already in this folder.

After that:

  * Plug in Kanishk's chip (the little board on the USB cable) and he pops up
    in the corner of the screen by himself.
  * Unplug it and he waves goodbye.
  * He starts with Windows from now on, quietly in the background, and waits
    for his chip. You never have to open anything again.

Talking to him:

  * Click him, or press Ctrl+Alt+Space and just talk ("Hey Kanishk, ...").
  * Right-click him for his wardrobe, tricks, modes and settings.

If you ever want him gone: double-click Uninstall.bat in this folder.

Made for Varuna.
